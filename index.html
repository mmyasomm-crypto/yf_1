Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Filters, presets, curves, color mix, crop, frames, background removal, AI masks and face enhance are processed on your device. Two features send the photo you are editing to the YoFilter AI server over the internet: Magic Eraser (the photo and the area you painted) and Portrait (the photo and your blur / skin settings). The server uses them only to create the result and returns it; the server software keeps them in memory and does not save your photos to disk. It may log technical details such as time, file size and IP address, never the photo itself. Background removal and AI masks run on your device but download their AI model from a public CDN the first time, unless the model is bundled with the app. We do not ask for an account or collect personal data. Exported photos are saved only on your device.
